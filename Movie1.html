TOP 5 most watched Indian film trailers on YouTube

1.Hi Nanna

Hi Nanna (2023)is a Telugu emotional romantic drama starring **Nani and Mrunal Thakur**. The story follows a loving father and his young daughter, whose lives change when a mysterious woman enters their world. It explores **love, family, memories, and relationships**, with an emotional twist.

2.Irumudi

Irumudi is a devotional story about Lord Ayyappa and the sacred journey of his devotees to Sabarimala. It beautifully portrays faith, devotion, friendship, sacrifice, and the spiritual challenges faced along the way. The story highlights the power of belief and the divine bond between devotees and Lord Ayyappa. 🙏🕉️

3.Geetha Govindam

Geetha Govindam is a romantic comedy about a kind-hearted young man, Govind, who falls in love with Geetha. A series of misunderstandings and funny situations bring them closer, leading to a heartwarming love story. ❤️🎬

4.Orange

Orange is a romantic drama about a man who believes that love should be lived in the present rather than promised forever. The film explores love, relationships, misunderstandings, and the emotional journey of two people with different views on love. ❤️🎬

5.Saaho

Saaho is an action thriller about a mysterious man caught in a dangerous battle involving crime, money, and betrayal. With thrilling action, twists, and a high-stakes mission, the story follows his fight against powerful enemies. 🎬🔥
